## About Section

### Why YaYa's Kitchen

YaYa's Kitchen is a brand that originated in 2011, built around the same guiding principles of nurturing and nourishing people.

The original expression of YaYa's Kitchen grew into a larger commercial food manufacturing experience. While our sauces were quite successful, the large-batch, wholesale world was not ultimately a good match for how we wanted to work or live.

After many years of experience, learning, and refinement, we believe this version of YaYa's Kitchen — small, local, and relationship-centered — is a much better fit for our values, our energy, and the kind of care we want to offer.

---

## How It Works

### A Simple Weekly Rhythm

YaYa's Kitchen follows a steady, predictable rhythm to maintain ease and flow — for us and for you. We believe this sense of calm carries from our kitchen to your family and table.

Each week's offerings are shared in advance. Orders are placed ahead of time so we can cook intentionally and avoid waste. Everything is prepared fresh, and baskets are delivered locally on Fridays.

---

## The Weekly Basket

### What's Inside

The YaYa's Kitchen basket is designed to support several meals — not replace grocery shopping.

It's meant to reduce decision-making, offer flexibility, and create a sense of being cared for.

Items rotate seasonally, and not everything appears every week.

**Core Kitchen Basket includes:**
- Breakfast item
- Soup
- Sandwich-style salad (think chicken or egg salad)
- Freshly baked bread

---

## Dinner Anchor

### One Complete Dinner Each Week

Each week includes one complete family dinner, which we call the Dinner Anchor.

**Boards** — Gather-and-eat style dinners. They include a protein, with several selections of meats and cheeses, vegetables, nuts, jams, and other tasty choices that feel generous and relaxed.

**Bowls** — Warm, composed meals built around a protein, vegetables, a grain, and a sauce.

**Traditional** — About once per month, the Dinner Anchor may be a traditional family meal — protein, grain or starch, and a vegetable side — for nights when simplicity is exactly what's needed.

*Only one Dinner Anchor is offered each week.*

## Food Values

### How We Cook

Our kitchen is guided by a few steady principles:

- Clean ingredients always
- Organic when possible
- Made fresh each week
- Seasonal choices prioritized
- Familiar food done well

We cook food people actually want to eat — nourishing, comforting, and thoughtfully prepared without unnecessary complication.

## For Travelers & Hosts

### Arrival Baskets

YaYa's Kitchen baskets are also available as a one-time option for guests staying in the Orlando area.

This is the same weekly basket we prepare for our local community — thoughtfully made, seasonal, and designed to help people land after travel.

Ideal for late arrivals, first mornings, or families who don't want to think right away.

### Optional: Basic Pantry Starter

A small set of everyday kitchen essentials to help you settle in — coffee or tea, eggs, milk, olive oil, and a few basics. Thoughtfully sourced and ready when you arrive.

---

## Containers & Delivery

### Thoughtful, Reusable Systems

Food is delivered in reusable baskets using glass containers and beeswax wraps whenever possible. Flowers are delivered in reusable vases.

A refundable container deposit carries forward week to week and helps keep the system simple, sustainable, and beautiful.

Weekly delivery is included for regular customers. Additional delivery for gift orders or arrival timing may include a fee.
