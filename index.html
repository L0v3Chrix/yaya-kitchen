Our story

Why YaYa's Kitchen

YaYa's Kitchen is a brand that originated in 2011, built around the same guiding principles of nurturing and nourishing people.

The original expression grew into a larger commercial food manufacturing experience. While our sauces were quite successful, the large-batch, wholesale world was not ultimately a good match for how we wanted to work or live.

After many years of experience, learning, and refinement, we believe this version of YaYa's Kitchen — small, local, and relationship-centered — is a much better fit for our values, our energy, and the kind of care we want to offer.

Where We Began

Before there were weekly baskets, there was sauce. YaYa's Original Pasta Sauce became a beloved product in local stores and farmers markets — proof that people craved food made with real care.

That same philosophy — cook with intention, use quality ingredients, nourish the people you're feeding — now lives in every basket we prepare.

Same values. Smaller scale. More personal.
