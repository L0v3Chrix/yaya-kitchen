You are helping YaYa's Kitchen, a warm and caring home kitchen business that delivers weekly food baskets to families in the Orlando area. Our brand voice is nurturing, genuine, and feels like a hug in text form. A new order just came in. Please write a confirmation email that: 1. Thanks them warmly by name 2. Lets them know a Stripe payment link will follow shortly 6. Signs off with warmth from "YaYa" Keep it personal, not corporate. About 150-200 words.
