🍳 Yaya's Stripe Setup Guide

Everything you need to do to accept payments for your cooking classes

📋 What This Guide Will Help You Do:
  • Make sure your Stripe account is fully set up
  • Find the special codes Chrix needs to connect your checkout
  • Set up your first product (the cooking class)

Time needed: About 30-45 minutes

Part 1: Log Into Your Stripe Account

1 Open Stripe in Your Web Browser

Go to: dashboard.stripe.com

You can click the link above, or type it into your browser's address bar at the top.

2 Sign In

Enter your email address and password that you used when you created your Stripe account.

💡 Tip: If you forgot your password, click "Forgot your password?" and Stripe will email you a reset link.

Part 2: Complete Your Account Setup

Before you can accept real payments, Stripe needs some information about you and your business. Let's make sure everything is filled in.

3 Check If Your Account Is Complete

Look at the top of your Stripe dashboard. Do you see any:

  • Yellow or orange banners saying "Complete your account"?
  • A progress bar that isn't at 100%?
  • Any warnings or "Action required" messages?

If you see any of these, click on them and follow Stripe's instructions to complete each section.

4 Go to Settings

Look in the bottom-left corner of the screen. You'll see a gear icon ⚙️ or the word Settings. Click it.

5 Check Your Business Details

In Settings, look for Business details or Account details and click it.

Make sure these are filled in:

  • ✅ Business name (can be "Yaya's Kitchen" or your legal name)
  • ✅ Business address
  • ✅ Phone number
  • ✅ Business type (probably "Individual" or "Sole proprietor")
6 Add Your Bank Account

This is where Stripe will send your money!

In Settings, look for Bank accounts and scheduling or Payouts. Click it.

If you haven't added a bank account yet:

  1. Click + Add bank account
  2. Enter your bank's routing number (9 digits)
  3. Enter your account number
  4. Confirm by entering the account number again
💡 Where to find these numbers: Look at the bottom of one of your checks. The routing number is the first set of 9 numbers. The account number is the second set.
7 Verify Your Identity

Stripe may ask you to verify your identity. This is normal and required by law.

Look in Settings for Identity verification or check if there's a banner asking for this.

You may need to provide:

  • Your Social Security Number (last 4 digits or full)
  • A photo of your driver's license or ID
  • Your date of birth
⚠️ This is safe: Stripe is a major payment company trusted by millions of businesses. They need this info to comply with banking laws.
✅ How to know Part 2 is complete:

When you go to your main dashboard, you should NOT see any warning banners at the top. If everything is green/clear at the top, you're good!

Part 3: Create Your Cooking Class Product

Now let's set up your cooking class as a product in Stripe so people can pay for it.

8 Go to Products

Look on the left side of your screen for the menu.

Click on Product catalog or just Products

(It might be under a section called "More" — click "More" if you don't see it)

9 Add Your First Product

Click the + Add product button (usually in the top right)

Fill in these details:

  • Name: Yaya's Cooking Class
  • Description: (optional) A fun, hands-on cooking class where you'll learn authentic family recipes!
10 Set the Price

Under "Pricing" fill in:

  • Price: Enter the amount (example: 75.00 for $75)
  • Currency: Make sure it says USD (US Dollars)
  • Type: Select "One time" (not recurring/subscription)

Then click Save product

✅ Your product is now created!

You should see "Yaya's Cooking Class" in your product list.

Part 4: Get the Information Chrix Needs

Now for the most important part — getting the special codes that let Chrix connect your website checkout to your Stripe account.

🔐 About These "Keys"

Stripe uses special codes called "API Keys" to securely connect your account to your website. Think of them like special passwords that let the website talk to Stripe.

You'll need to find 2 keys:

  • The "Publishable key" (this one is okay to share)
  • The "Secret key" (keep this private — only share with Chrix)
11 Go to the Developers Section

Look on the left side menu for Developers

Click it to expand the menu, then click API keys

Alternative path: You can also click the gear ⚙️ (Settings) → then look for "Developers" → "API keys"

12 Make Sure You're in LIVE Mode (Not Test Mode)

This is very important!

Look at the top of your Stripe dashboard. You might see a toggle or switch that says:

  • "Test mode" (with an orange/yellow indicator) — ❌ NOT this one
  • "Live mode" (no special color) — ✅ This is what we want

If you see "Test mode" is on, click the toggle to switch to Live mode.

⚠️ Why this matters: Test mode keys won't accept real payments. We need the live keys!
13 Find and Copy Your Publishable Key

On the API keys page, you'll see a section called "Standard keys"

Look for "Publishable key" — it starts with pk_live_

Click the copy button (looks like two overlapping squares 📋) next to it.

Paste it somewhere safe (like a note on your phone or a text message to yourself).

14 Find and Copy Your Secret Key

Look for "Secret key" — it will say "Reveal live key" or show sk_live_

Click Reveal live key (you might need to re-enter your password)

Once revealed, click the copy button 📋 next to it.

Paste it somewhere safe with the publishable key.

🔒 Keep this secret! Never post this key publicly or share it with anyone except Chrix. It gives full access to your Stripe account.
15 Get Your Product Price ID

Go back to Product catalog → Products

Click on "Yaya's Cooking Class" (the product you created)

Scroll down to the "Pricing" section

You'll see your price listed. Look for a code that starts with price_

It looks something like: price_1ABC123def456...

Click to copy this Price ID.

💡 Can't find it? Click on the price row itself — a side panel might open showing the Price ID.

Part 5: Send Everything to Chrix

📤 Copy and Send These 3 Things to Chrix:

Send a message to Chrix with:

  1. Publishable Key (starts with pk_live_)
  2. Secret Key (starts with sk_live_)
  3. Price ID (starts with price_)

You can text these, or send via WhatsApp — however you normally communicate.

💡 Easy way to organize your message:

Here are my Stripe keys for the checkout:

Publishable Key: pk_live_xxxxx
Secret Key: sk_live_xxxxx
Price ID: price_xxxxx

✅ Final Checklist

Before You're Done, Make Sure:

Account is fully verified — No warning banners at the top of your dashboard
Bank account is connected — So you can receive your money!
Product is created — "Yaya's Cooking Class" shows in your products
You copied the LIVE keys — Not test keys! (pk_live and sk_live, not pk_test)
All 3 items sent to Chrix — Publishable key, Secret key, and Price ID

🎉 You Did It!

Once Chrix receives your keys, he'll connect everything and you'll be ready to start accepting payments for your cooking classes!

Questions? Just text or call Chrix.
